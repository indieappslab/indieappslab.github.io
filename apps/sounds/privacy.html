Privacy Policy – Soundia

Last updated: 2026-10-27

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Soundia: Sleep, Focus & Relax mobile application (“Soundia”, “the App”) developed by Indie Apps Lab (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information.

Soundia is an ambient sound application designed for sleep, focus and relaxation. It lets you combine sound layers, choose soundscapes, save a custom mix and set a listening timer. Its audio is generated by the App rather than streamed from an online music service.

We do not require an account, track your listening behavior or upload your personal sound mixes to Indie Apps Lab servers. Certain optional features, especially scene photographs and external support links, can involve other services as described below.

2. Key Points at a Glance

  • No accounts. No registration or sign-in is required.
  • No behavioral tracking. The App does not integrate analytics or advertising identifiers.
  • No ads. The App does not include advertising SDKs.
  • Local preferences. Your sound mix, scene selection and timer settings are stored on your device.
  • No developer-operated cloud sync. We do not upload your mixes or preferences to our servers.
  • No microphone recording. The App produces audio; it does not record your voice or surroundings.
  • Offline audio. Sound playback works without an internet connection; optional scene photographs require network access when not cached.

3. Data Stored by the App

3.1 Sound Mixes & Playback Preferences (Local Storage)

  • The App saves your selected soundscape, the volume levels of individual sound layers, your chosen session timer and any custom mix you save.
  • These settings are kept locally on your device using the App’s private preferences storage.
  • The App does not require your name, email address or phone number to save these settings. We do not receive them on Indie Apps Lab servers.
  • Soundia does not currently create a listening history, user profile or cloud-based record of your sessions.

3.2 Audio Generation

Soundia generates and mixes its ambient audio on your device. The App does not transmit microphone input, voice recordings or audio from your environment to us. Its core sound playback does not require an internet connection.

3.3 Information You Send to Support

Contacting support is optional. If you choose to send an email, your email address and the information you include will be processed by your email provider and our email provider so that we can read and respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive information unless necessary.

4. Device Permissions

Soundia uses technical capabilities needed for playing audio, including background playback and playback controls while the screen is locked. Depending on your operating system, these may involve internet access, foreground media playback, wake-lock functionality and system media notifications or controls.

The App’s current features do not require access to:

  • Microphone or camera.
  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Contacts, call history or SMS messages.
  • Your photo library or external file storage.

Internet access is used to request optional scene photographs and to open external links when you choose to use them. The “Contact support” action opens your email application; the Privacy Policy action opens your browser. If a suitable app is unavailable, Soundia may copy the relevant email address or link to your clipboard. It does not read the contents of your clipboard.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

5.1 Optional Unsplash Photographs

Soundia may request scene photographs from Unsplash through unsplash.com and its image delivery service, including images.unsplash.com. When your device requests an image, the image provider may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request, such as your IP address, request time and browser or device-related request headers. The provider may process that information under its own privacy practices.

These requests are for scene artwork, not for streaming audio or transmitting your saved mixes. Images may be cached by your device or the operating system. If you are offline or an image cannot load, the App can display its built-in visual background instead; sound playback remains available offline. See the Unsplash Privacy Policy for information about its processing practices.

5.2 Email, Browser & App Distribution Services

If you open the Privacy Policy in a browser or send a support email, the browser, email provider and destination service may handle information according to their own privacy policies. App stores, operating systems and device manufacturers may also process installation, diagnostic or account-related information independently of Soundia.

5.3 Analytics, Advertising & Crash Reporting

The App does not currently integrate advertising networks, behavioral analytics or developer-operated crash-reporting SDKs. We do not use Soundia to build advertising profiles.

6. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information or saved sound mixes.
  • We do not share your locally saved mixes with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not upload your listening preferences to Indie Apps Lab servers.
  • When optional photographs load, the photo provider receives the associated network request as explained in Section 5.
  • Support correspondence is processed by the email providers involved and may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your selected soundscape, sound levels, saved mix and timer preferences remain in local App storage until they are changed or the App’s data is cleared or removed from that installation.
  • Uninstalling Soundia or clearing its application storage generally removes its local preferences from that installation.
  • Android, iOS or account-level backup services may separately store copies of some local data if enabled on your device. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform provider and your settings, not by Indie Apps Lab.
  • Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, keep relevant support records or comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion of your correspondence using the contact details below, subject to applicable law.

8. Children’s Privacy

Soundia does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction). The App does not require an account or the submission of personal information to use its sound features.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR where applicable, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you.

  • Access and control. Your sound settings and saved mix are available through the App on your device.
  • Deletion. You can clear the App’s local data or uninstall it; review system backup settings separately if needed.
  • Support correspondence. You can contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information provided in support emails, where applicable.
  • Third-party requests. For information processed by Unsplash, an email provider or an app store, consult that provider’s privacy policy and rights-request process.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this policy when Soundia’s features, services or legal obligations change.
  • The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates its current version.
  • Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.
