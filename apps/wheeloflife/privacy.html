Privacy Policy – Wheel of Life

Last updated: 2026-10-25

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wheel of Life: Balance Tracker mobile application (“Wheel of Life”, “the App”) developed by Indie Apps Lab (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles your information.

Wheel of Life is a personal reflection application that helps you assess different areas of your life, visualize your balance with classic wheel and radar charts, and review changes over time.

Privacy and simplicity are core principles of the App. We do not require accounts, collect your assessments on our servers, or track how you use the App.

2. Key Points at a Glance

  • No accounts. No registration or sign-in is required.
  • No tracking. No behavioral analytics or advertising identifiers are used by the App.
  • No ads. The App does not include advertising SDKs.
  • Local storage. Your assessments and reflection notes are saved in the App’s private storage on your device.
  • No developer-operated cloud sync. We do not upload your life-balance records to our servers.
  • Offline functionality. The App’s assessment, chart and history features work without an internet connection.

3. Data Stored by the App

3.1 Life Areas, Assessments & Reflection Notes (Local Storage)

  • The App stores the life areas you use, including their names, colors and order; your satisfaction ratings (from 1 to 10); assessment dates; optional reflection notes; and a history of previous assessments.
  • This information is stored locally on your device in the App’s private SQLite database. Previous assessments retain snapshots of their life-area names, colors and ratings.
  • We do not transmit your assessments, ratings or notes to Indie Apps Lab servers or provide an in-app cloud synchronization service.
  • Reflection notes are optional. Please remember that you control the information you choose to enter, which may be personal or sensitive to you.

3.2 App Settings

  • Preferences such as the light, dark or system theme and the selected chart type (classic wheel or radar) are stored on your device.
  • These settings are not transmitted to us by the App.

3.3 Information You Send to Support

The App does not require your name, email address or phone number to function. If you voluntarily contact support, your email address and any information you include in your message will be received and processed through the email service you use and our email provider so we can respond to your request.

Please do not include sensitive life assessments, personal notes or other confidential information in support messages unless necessary for your request.

4. Device Permissions

Wheel of Life is designed to function without requesting sensitive device permissions. Its core features do not need access to:

  • Camera or microphone.
  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Contacts or call history.
  • Photos, media library or external file storage.

The optional “Contact support” action asks your device to open a configured email application. If none is available, it can copy the support email address to your clipboard. The App does not read your clipboard contents.

5. Third-Party Services & Device Backups

The App does not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, tracking frameworks or developer-operated crash-reporting services. Its core functionality does not require network access.

The Android operating system and your device manufacturer or account provider may offer separate system-level backup and restore functionality. Depending on your device and backup settings, some locally stored App data may be included in those backups and processed by the relevant platform provider. Such services are not operated by Indie Apps Lab. You can review the backup settings of your device.

If you choose to email support, your email application and email providers process the message under their own privacy terms.

6. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your assessment data or personal reflection notes.
  • We do not share your locally stored life-balance records with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not transfer your life-balance records to our own servers.
  • Support emails are handled through email service providers and may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Assessments, notes, life-area configurations and preferences remain in the App’s local storage until you delete the relevant data or clear the App’s storage.
  • You can delete individual saved assessments through the App’s history screen.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage generally removes its local data from that installation. Copies in any system-level backups, if enabled, are controlled through your device or backup provider’s settings and policies.
  • If you email support, we retain the correspondence only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records or comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the contact details below, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

Wheel of Life does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction). The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, including under the GDPR where applicable, you may have rights concerning the personal data we process.

  • Access. Your assessments and notes are available in the App on your device.
  • Deletion. Delete individual assessments in the App or clear the App’s local storage.
  • Control. Edit your life areas and choose what information to record.
  • Contact. For requests about support correspondence or questions about this policy, email us using the address below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy as features, services or legal requirements change.
  • The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will indicate the current version.
  • Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
