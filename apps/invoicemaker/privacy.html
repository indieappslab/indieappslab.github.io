Privacy Policy – Invoice Maker

Last updated: 2026-10-29

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Invoice Maker mobile application ("Invoice Maker", "the App") developed by Indie Apps Lab ("we", "us", "our") handles information.

Invoice Maker lets you create, manage, preview, export and share invoices and estimates, organize clients and products or services, and maintain business details.

Privacy is a core principle of the App. Business information and document contents are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud service that stores or synchronizes your invoice or client database. Certain optional functions, explained below, may contact third-party services.

2. Key Points at a Glance

  • No accounts. No registration or developer-operated sign-in.
  • No tracking. No advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.
  • No ads. The App does not include advertising SDKs.
  • No automatic cloud sync. Invoices, estimates and client records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
  • Optional backups. You control when and where files are exported.
  • Offline core features. Document creation and editing work locally; font downloads and optional Google Play purchase services may require internet access.

3. Data Stored by the App

3.1 Business Profile (Local Storage)

  • You may enter a business name, address, email, phone number, website, tax identification number and bank or payment details.
  • These details are stored locally and may appear in documents you create.
  • We do not automatically upload this information to our servers.

3.2 Clients, Invoices & Estimates (Local Storage)

  • Client records may contain names, contact details, addresses and tax identification numbers.
  • Documents may contain numbers, dates, descriptions, quantities, prices, currencies, taxes, discounts, payment details, notes and statuses.
  • These records are saved in the App's private local database, not on our servers.
  • Some of this content is personal data. You are responsible for having an appropriate basis to record and share client information.

3.3 Products, Services & App Settings

  • Product and service names, descriptions, units and prices are stored locally.
  • Language, currency, appearance and locally cached purchase entitlement preferences are stored on your device.

3.4 Information We Receive

We do not receive your invoice or client database through normal document editing. If you contact support, we receive what you voluntarily send. If paid features become available, limited purchase identifiers and verification information may be processed to verify your purchase, as described below.

4. PDF Exports, Sharing & Backups

  • PDF documents: You can generate, preview, save, print or share invoices and estimates.
  • Sharing: If you send a document through email, messaging, a cloud provider or a print service, your chosen recipient or service may process its contents under its own privacy terms.
  • Backups: You can export and restore your data as JSON files using the device's file picker.
  • Important: Exported JSON backups are not encrypted by the App and may contain client contact details, business information, tax identifiers and bank details. Store these files securely.
  • We do not automatically upload exported PDFs or backup files to our servers. Files saved outside the App remain under your control and that of the selected service.

5. Device Permissions

The App uses limited device functions needed for its features.

  • Internet: May be used by optional Google Play purchase services, purchase verification, online links and downloads of fonts used for PDFs.
  • Files: The Android file picker and share or print interfaces let you choose documents to import, export or share; the App does not require unrestricted access to device storage.
  • Camera, microphone and location: Not required for the App's current functionality.
  • Contacts: The App does not read your device's address book. Client information is entered manually.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Google Play Billing

The App includes support for optional purchases and subscriptions through Google Play. When purchasing is available, Google handles payment information under its own policies. We do not directly receive your full payment card details.

If purchase verification is enabled, the App may send a product identifier, purchase identifier and purchase verification token to a secure verification service to validate entitlement. Your invoices and client records are not needed for that process.

6.2 PDF Font Resources

When generating PDF files, the App may download font resources from Google-hosted font services. Requests may expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to the service provider. Invoice or client contents are not intentionally included in font requests.

6.3 Analytics, Advertising & Crash Reporting

The current App does not integrate third-party analytics, targeted advertising, tracking frameworks or crash-reporting SDKs.

Google services process information under their own terms. See Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data or document contents.
  • We do not use your information for targeted advertising.
  • We do not automatically share your invoices, estimates or client records with third parties.
  • Limited technical or purchase information may be processed by Google Play, font providers, or an enabled purchase verification service.
  • Files you choose to share or export may be handled by third parties, including services located abroad.
  • We may disclose information where legally required.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data generally remains on your device until you delete it where the App permits, clear the App's storage or uninstall it.
  • You may edit business and client information. Some issued documents cannot be individually deleted within the App.
  • PDFs and backups exported outside the App are not automatically removed on uninstall. Delete copies separately from the device, cloud service or recipient where they are stored.
  • Google Play payment records and records maintained by a configured verification service may have separate retention periods for billing, security and legal purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Invoice Maker is a business productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, including the GDPR where relevant, you may have data protection rights.

  • Access and correction: Review and edit data stored locally on your device.
  • Deletion: Delete supported records, clear App storage or uninstall the App; remove exported files separately.
  • Control: Choose if and where to share or back up your documents.
  • Requests: Contact us about information, if any, processed through support correspondence or an enabled purchase verification service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy if the App, its integrations or applicable requirements change.
  • The “Last updated” date reflects the latest policy revision.
