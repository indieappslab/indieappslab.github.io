Privacy Policy – Invoice Maker

Last updated: 2026-10-30

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Invoice Maker mobile application ("Invoice Maker", "the App") developed by Indie Apps Lab ("we", "us", "our") handles information.

Invoice Maker lets you create, manage, preview, export and share invoices and estimates, organize clients and products or services, and maintain business details.

Privacy is a core principle of the App. Business information and document contents are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud service that stores or synchronizes your invoice or client database. The App does not automatically transmit invoice or client records to us. If you choose to export, share or print a document, other apps or services you select may handle it.

2. Key Points at a Glance

  • No accounts. No registration or developer-operated sign-in is required.
  • No analytics or tracking. The App does not include advertising, behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No ads or subscriptions. This version does not include advertising, in-app purchases or paid subscriptions.
  • Local storage. Invoices, estimates, client records and business details are stored in the App's local database.
  • No developer cloud sync. We do not automatically upload or synchronize your documents or client database to our servers. Android operating-system backups may be available according to your device settings.
  • Offline documents and fonts. Invoice and estimate creation, editing and PDF generation use local resources, including fonts packaged with the App.
  • Exports are your choice. You decide when to save, share, print or back up your information.

3. Data Stored by the App

3.1 Business Profile (Local Storage)

  • You may enter a business name, address, email, phone number, website, tax identification number and bank or payment details.
  • These details are stored locally and may appear in documents you create.
  • We do not automatically upload this information to our servers.

3.2 Clients, Invoices & Estimates (Local Storage)

  • Client records may contain names, contact details, addresses and tax identification numbers.
  • Documents may contain numbers, dates, descriptions, quantities, prices, currencies, taxes, discounts, payment details, notes and statuses.
  • These records are saved in the App's private local database, not on our servers.
  • Some of this content is personal data. You are responsible for having an appropriate basis to record and share client information.

3.3 Products, Services & App Settings

  • Product and service names, descriptions, units and prices are stored locally.
  • Language, currency and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device.

3.4 Information We Receive

We do not receive your invoice or client database through normal use of the App. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information or files you choose to include in your message. Email providers may process that correspondence according to their own privacy policies.

4. PDF Exports, Sharing & Backups

  • PDF documents: You can generate and preview invoices and estimates using fonts bundled with the App. PDF generation does not require downloading fonts from the internet.
  • Saving and sharing: You may save, print or share PDFs using Android's file picker, sharing features, supported printing services or other apps you choose.
  • Third-party destinations: If you send a document through email, messaging, a cloud-storage provider or a printing service, that recipient or service may process its contents under its own privacy terms.
  • Backups: You can manually export and restore app data as JSON files using the device's file picker.
  • Important: Exported JSON backup files are not encrypted by the App. They may contain client contact details, business information, tax identifiers and bank details. Store them securely and share them only with trusted recipients.
  • Android system backups: Depending on your device configuration, Android or your device provider may back up app data separately from the App's manual export feature. These backups are controlled by your device and operating-system settings, not by Indie Apps Lab.
  • We do not automatically upload exported PDFs or manual backup files to our servers. Copies saved or shared outside the App must be managed separately.

5. Device Permissions and External Apps

The App uses device functions needed to create and manage documents.

  • Internet: An internet connection is not needed to create, edit or generate PDFs, and the App does not download PDF fonts or contact a subscription verification service. Links opened in an external browser, email support, online sharing and some printing services may use the internet.
  • Files: Android's file picker and share or print interfaces let you choose which files to import, save or share. The App does not require unrestricted access to your device's storage.
  • Camera, microphone and location: The App does not require access to these device features.
  • Contacts: The App does not read your device's address book. Client details are entered manually.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Analytics, Advertising, Crash Reporting and Payments

This version of the App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not use Google Play Billing or a purchase-verification server.

6.2 PDF Fonts

PDF fonts are bundled with the App and loaded from its installed assets. The App does not download PDF fonts from Google Fonts or other online font providers when you create documents.

6.3 Services You Choose to Open

If you open our Privacy Policy or support link, or use another app to share, save or print a document, your browser, email provider, cloud-storage provider or printing service may process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those external services.

7. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data or document contents.
  • We do not use your information for targeted advertising.
  • We do not automatically send invoices, estimates, client records or business details to our servers or share them with third parties.
  • When you choose to export, print, share or back up information through another app or service, that destination may receive and process the files you select.
  • Information you voluntarily send to support is received and processed as part of that communication.
  • We may disclose information that we actually hold if legally required to do so.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data remains on your device until you remove it using available features, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, subject to Android's backup and restore behavior.
  • You may edit business and client information. Some issued documents cannot be deleted individually within the App; clearing the App's storage removes the local database.
  • PDFs and JSON backups exported outside the App are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the App. Remove copies separately from your device, any cloud storage or recipients.
  • If you contact support, we may retain the correspondence as needed to respond to your request, manage support inquiries or meet applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to request deletion of information we hold, subject to applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Invoice Maker is a business productivity tool intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed or marketed for children. The App does not operate a developer-managed account system or intentionally collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, including the GDPR where relevant, you may have data protection rights.

  • Access and correction: Review and update the information stored locally in the App.
  • Deletion: Delete supported records, clear App storage or uninstall the App. Remove exported files and operating-system backups separately where applicable.
  • Control: Choose if, when and where to export, print, share or manually back up your documents.
  • Support inquiries: Contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of any personal information we may hold through support correspondence, subject to applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy if the App, its integrations or applicable requirements change.
  • The “Last updated” date reflects the latest policy revision.
